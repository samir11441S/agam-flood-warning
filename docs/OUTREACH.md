# Outreach scripts — the things only your team can do

## 1. Union Parishad / Union Disaster Management Committee (visit or phone)

**Goal:** understand how they warn people today, and get one quote or a letter of intent.

> "আসসালামু আলাইকুম / নমস্কার। আমরা [বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়]-এর শিক্ষার্থী, 'আগাম' নামে একটি বন্যা সতর্কবার্তা ব্যবস্থা তৈরি করছি — সীমান্তের ওপারের বৃষ্টি দেখে আগেভাগে সতর্ক করে, আর ইউনিয়নের প্রতিটি পরিবারে ভয়েস কল ও এসএমএস পাঠাতে সাহায্য করে। আপনাদের অভিজ্ঞতা জানতে ১৫ মিনিট সময় চাই।"

Questions:
1. How did you hear about the August 2024 (or a recent) flood, and when?
2. How do you warn villagers today? Who does it? How long does it take?
3. Who is hardest to reach (elderly, disabled, char, no phone)?
4. Do you keep household lists? Do they include phone numbers?
5. Would a tool like this help? What would stop you from using it?
6. Could you write a short letter saying you'd be interested in a pilot? (template in [BUSINESS.md](BUSINESS.md))

Write down exact quotes (with permission) — judges value them.

## 3. NRB expert (LinkedIn / email) — counts toward the 10% NRB criterion

Look for Bangladeshi hydrologists, flood forecasters, disaster-risk or climate-data researchers abroad (universities, World Bank, UN, ECMWF, NASA, consultancies).

> Assalamu alaikum / Hello [name],
> We're a Bangladeshi student team building "Agam", a flash-flood early-warning prototype for the International AI Builders Congress. It uses upstream rainfall across the border, local 30-year thresholds and hourly data; our evaluation is public on GitHub, including our misses.
> Would you be willing to spend 30 minutes reviewing our method (thresholds, travel times, evaluation) and, if you find it worthwhile, be listed as an advisor? Your expertise would make a real difference.

## 4. Telecom operator (CSR / enterprise team)

> **Subject:** Flood early-warning pilot — voice and SMS partner
>
> Agam sends Bangla voice calls and SMS to households in flood-prone unions, approved by local disaster committees. We are looking for a partner for bulk voice/SMS (and, longer term, Cell Broadcast) for a one-union pilot during the next monsoon. We can share our cost model and evaluation.

## 5. What to record for the submission
- Number of conversations, who (role, not names unless they agree), and the key quotes.
- Any letter of intent (scan/photo).
- Field-test results ([FIELD-TEST.md](FIELD-TEST.md)).
